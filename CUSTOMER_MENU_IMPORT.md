# BATA KADE owner menu

Updated 17 November 2026 from the owner list supplied in chat. `customer_menu.json` is the canonical import; the static preview embeds the same menu.

Breakfast items are breakfast-only. Lunch and Kottu are offered at lunch/dinner; dinner availability needs owner confirmation. Drinks and desserts are offered in every meal. Biryani, Cream Soda 1L and Ginger Beer 1L are visible but disabled until prices are confirmed. Milk Tea is Rs.100 with its type marked to confirm. Older products absent from the new list are removed. Codes 311/312 now mean Vegetable Fried Rice, replacing the former Egg Fried Rice.

Existing local databases are not overwritten. The static site remains a demo, with no real payments.
